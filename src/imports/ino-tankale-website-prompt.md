## About the business

Ino Tankale is a UK-based (Newmarket, Suffolk) business consultancy offering two service lines:

1. **Fractional / Outsourced COO Services** — Working as an embedded strategic partner for small and growing businesses. Focused on strategic execution, plan accountability, and helping business owners actually deliver on their revenue-generating commitments, not just plan them. Ideal for founders who have a strategy but lack the operational bandwidth or discipline to execute it consistently.

2. **Social Media Management** — Managing and growing social media presence for businesses and public-facing individuals (e.g. churches, ministries, entrepreneurs). Covers content planning, consistent posting across existing platforms, and a long-term goal of reducing how much day-to-day work the client has to do themselves, freeing them up to show up only for the highest-value moments.

**Target clients:** Small business owners, founders, and public figures (e.g. church leaders, entrepreneurs) who need either operational accountability or consistent, professional social media presence, but don't have the internal team to do it themselves.
